Local processing

HealthShare processes your selected health data on this device. It has no account, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics. HealthShare does not send your health data to the developer or operate a health-data server.

Apple Health access

With your permission, HealthShare reads selected nutrition, activity, weight, waist, sleep and state-of-mind records to display them and prepare JSON exports. It does not write, change or delete records in Apple Health. Availability depends on your permissions and the data supplied by your health apps.

Copying and sharing

Copy places the selected JSON on the system clipboard. System features such as Universal Clipboard may make it available on your other devices. Share creates a protected temporary JSON file and opens the system share sheet. Only choose destinations you trust: exported files can contain sensitive health data and source-provided notes. Copies saved or sent to other apps remain under your control and those services’ privacy policies.

Storage and control

Read results are held in memory, not in a separate persistent health database. Temporary share files are removed when the share sheet closes; leftover files older than 24 hours are removed when the app starts or prepares another share. This is not a guarantee of deletion while the app is closed. Clipboard content and copies you save elsewhere are not removed by HealthShare. You can change HealthShare’s access in Apple Health or iOS Settings.

Website hosting

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes and may process additional technical access data under its privacy statement. Health records from the app are not sent to this website.

We add no analytics, tracking scripts, forms or cookies to this website. Fonts are system fonts; images and styles are served by the same host. GitHub may process data outside the EU. Its privacy statement explains recipients, transfer mechanisms and retention. The Pages documentation describes IP logging. Our legal basis for providing and securing the website is legitimate interests under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR.

Email support

When you contact us, we use your email address and message to handle your request. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR for requests concerning our services, otherwise Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (responding to inquiries). We delete correspondence when it is no longer needed unless statutory retention obligations apply. Please do not include health records in your initial message.

Your rights

Where we process personal data, you have rights under the GDPR to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection, subject to its conditions. Consent may be withdrawn for the future. You may complain to a data protection authority, particularly the Saxon Data Protection and Transparency Commissioner. Contact info@eggnition.com for requests.
