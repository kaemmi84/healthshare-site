Here to help.

Questions about HealthShare? Email info@eggnition.com with your iOS version and a description of the issue. Please do not include health records or exports in your initial message.

Why are records missing?

HealthShare can only read records other apps provide to Apple Health and that you allow it to access. Check the source in Apple Health and the permissions for HealthShare.

What is stored?

Analysis runs locally. The app has no account, advertising or tracking. You control what you copy or share.
